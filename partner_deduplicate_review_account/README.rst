Installed automatically with *Contact Deduplication Review* and
*Invoicing*.

A contact merge that would move **posted journal items between two
different customers** is refused: merging two customers rewrites one
ledger into the other, including the items of the merged customer's
contacts, whose commercial entity changes with it. That is a decision
for accounting.

Contacts of the same customer merge as usual. To allow a cross-customer
merge deliberately, pass ``deduplicate_allow_ledger_move=True`` in the
context.
